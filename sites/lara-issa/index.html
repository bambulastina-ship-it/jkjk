01 About

I have been working in Dubai real estate since 2019 — long enough to know which buildings hold their value, which handovers run late, and which questions to ask before anyone signs anything.

Most of the people I work with are moving with intent: a family upgrading from an apartment to a villa community, an owner deciding whether this is the right season to list, a professional relocating to Dubai who needs a lease sorted before they land. The market here moves quickly and it is noisy — my job is to reduce it to the handful of options that actually fit you, and to tell you plainly when something does not.

I work directly, not through a call centre. You speak to me on WhatsApp, you view with me, and I stay on it through the paperwork — the NOC, the transfer appointment, the Ejari, the handover. The first conversation is free and there is no obligation attached to it; if I am not the right person for what you need, I will say so.

02 Services

Three ways I work

I

Buying

We start with the brief — budget, area, handover timing, whether it is to live in or to hold. I shortlist, I arrange the viewings, and I come with you. You get the honest version of each unit: the layout that looks bigger in photos than it lives, the floor that catches the afternoon sun, the service charge that changes the maths.

  • Ready and off-plan across Dubai
  • Viewings arranged and attended with you
  • Offer, MOU and transfer walked through end to end

II

Selling

Before anything is listed we agree on a realistic asking price and on how the property should be presented — what to fix, what to clear, how it photographs. Then I market it, I qualify the enquiries so your home is not a weekend attraction, and I bring you offers with the buyer's position attached, not just a number.

  • Pricing conversation grounded in current comparables
  • Presentation and photography guidance before listing
  • Buyers qualified before a single viewing is booked

III

Leasing

For tenants, leasing in Dubai is a race against good stock and a short decision window — so I keep the shortlist tight and move fast on the paperwork. For landlords, I look for a tenant who will renew: the right cheque structure, the right profile, a lease that does not come back to you in six months.

  • Shortlists filtered to what is genuinely available now
  • Cheque terms and lease clauses explained before you sign
  • Ejari and handover coordinated on both sides

04 Process

How working together goes

  1. 01

    The free consultation

    A conversation on WhatsApp or a call. What you want, what you can move on, and by when. No documents needed at this stage and nothing to sign.

  2. 02

    A shortlist, not a catalogue

    I come back with a small number of options that fit the brief, each with the reason it is on the list — and the reason it might not be for you.

  3. 03

    Viewings and the real numbers

    We view together. Service charges, chiller, cheque structure, agency and transfer costs — everything on the table before an offer goes anywhere.

  4. 04

    Paperwork to keys

    Offer, MOU or tenancy contract, NOC, transfer or Ejari, handover. I stay with it to the end and you always know what the next step is.
