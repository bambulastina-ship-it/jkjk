Drop the studio photos in this folder.

Filenames matter - the site looks for these exact names:

  logo.png      - logo (transparent PNG if possible)
  hero.jpg      - big hero photo, landscape
  owner.jpg     - gym master / owner portrait
  gym-01.jpg    - gym / workout photos, keep numbering up to gym-08.jpg

Lowercase filenames. Anything you skip simply won't show.
