## COMPLIANCE FLAG — must be resolved by client
Her bio reads: "Will double your money same way I did with mine".

Do not put this on the website as written. It is an explicit financial return
promise. Three problems:
  1. UAE property marketing is regulated (RERA/DLD). Guaranteed-return claims in
     real estate advertising are a compliance exposure for her, not for us.
  2. It is an unverifiable performance claim — the brief bans fake guarantees
     and metrics, and we cannot substantiate this one.
  3. To an actual HNW investor it reads as a red flag, not a hook. Sophisticated
     buyers discount anyone promising to double their money.

Compliant alternatives that keep the investor-led edge:
  - "Dubai property, bought as an investment — not as a home."
  - "Off-market and distressed opportunities, sourced before they list."
  - "I invest in this market myself. I advise on it the same way."
Final wording is the client's call. Escalate before shipping.
