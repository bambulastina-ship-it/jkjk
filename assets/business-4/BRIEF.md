## Brand
The logo is a strong, complete, black-and-white circular mark: "The Stroppy Cow"
arched over a halftone photograph of a Highland cow, "Biscuit Company" arched
beneath, with the Instagram handle set below. The name has real personality —
dry British humour. The copy should have a bit of that character without
tipping into gimmick.

This logo is the best Liquid Metal input across all five businesses: already
high-contrast black on white, already circular. Use the real file.

## CTA direction
Everything runs through enquiry and consultation. Primary CTA: **Enquire /
start a consultation**, with the occasion, date and rough quantity. Secondary:
the Instagram gallery, which is where the work actually lives.

⚠️ The £35 minimum, the lead time and postal delivery make this the site most
likely to drift toward a shop. It must NOT. State the minimum and lead time as
plain information so enquiries arrive well-qualified — no basket, no ordering,
no deposits, no payment of any kind.
