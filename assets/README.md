# Image drop zone

Build is BLOCKED until real business imagery is supplied. No stock, no AI-generated
placeholders — the supplied photography drives the design direction for each site.

Drop each business's photos into its own folder:

- `assets/business-1/` — Business 1
- `assets/business-2/` — Business 2
- `assets/business-3/` — Business 3

Per business, the useful minimum is:
- 1–2 wide hero shots (landscape, high resolution)
- 4–8 gallery / portfolio / product / interior shots
- 1–2 section-background or atmosphere shots
- logo (SVG or transparent PNG) if one exists

Keep original resolution — cropping and art direction happen in code, not before upload.
